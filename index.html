How do you structure your projects

I work on a project-by-project basis with a clear, fixed scope. This ensures we are both aligned on the final vision, the deadline, and the total cost from the very beginning.

How long does a typical project take?

For a standard landing page or portfolio, the turnaround is usually 1–2 weeks. More complex web applications or multi-page sites typically take 3–5 weeks depending on the complexity of the features

Can you really build "any" front-end design?

Yes. Whether you have a high-fidelity Figma file, a hand-drawn sketch, or just a rough idea, I specialize in the technical execution required to make those visions a functional reality using React and Next.js.

Do you handle the UI/UX design or just the code?

I do both. I can work from your existing designs, or I can design the interface from scratch. My goal is to ensure the site looks beautiful while being engineered for high performance.


What is your technical stack?

My core stack is React and Next.js. I use Tailwind CSS for clean, responsive styling and Framer Motion for smooth, high-end animations. For the backend, I can integrate with various APIs or CMS platforms.

Can you make my site fast and SEO-friendly?

Yes, Every project includes a support window for bug fixes and minor tweaks. If you need ongoing updates or new features down the road, I’m available for continued collaboration on a freelance basis
